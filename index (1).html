How it works

DRAW
YOUR PATH.

Drag your finger to draw a line. The ball rolls along whatever you draw. That's the whole game, and it never gets old.

01 — DRAW

Draw the road

One finger, one line. Sketch ramps, bridges and loops ahead of the ball, live, while it rolls.

02 — ROLL

Keep it rolling

Real physics: slopes speed you up, climbs slow you down. Momentum is your friend, until it isn't.

03 — GO FURTHER

Go one meter further

Every run is measured in meters. Your best stays on screen, waiting for you to beat it.

The challenge

THINK
BEFORE YOU DRAW.

Walls, gaps, spikes and things that fly. Plan one line that clears them all, and watch your ink: it doesn't last forever.

Walls

RAMP OVER

Draw a ramp early enough to clear them. Too late and you bounce right back.

Gaps

BRIDGE IT

The road ends. Yours doesn't. Sketch a bridge before the ball runs out of ground.

Spikes

ONE TOUCH

Touch them and the run burns. Arc over or bridge across, never through.

Paper planes

DODGE

They glide in from the side. Time your line low, or let them pass first.

Gold

COLLECT

Worth the detour, mostly. Draw through the coins for a richer run.

Ink

SPEND WISELY

Every line costs ink. Long scribbles get you nowhere. Short, smart lines win runs.

Lives

EVERY
LIFE
COUNTS.

Three tries per run. Fall or burn and you respawn with a little extra ink. Lose them all and the run is over.
